Privacy Policy

App: SutraHealth WhatsApp Demo (Sutra_Demo) · Developer: Sachin Choudhary · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This is a demonstration WhatsApp assistant used to showcase a preventive-health workflow. It is not a medical service and does not give medical advice.

What we collect

When you message the bot we receive your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, the messages and button choices you send, and the partner store code if you arrive through a store QR code.

How we use it

Only to reply to you inside the chat, calculate the demo health-check result and show anonymous counts (scans, chats, check-ins, bookings) on an internal dashboard. Phone numbers are masked on that dashboard.

Storage and retention

Demo data is kept only in server memory and is erased whenever the demo server restarts. We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which handles data under Meta's own policies.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time. To ask about or delete your data, contact the developer through sachinchoudhary07.github.io.
